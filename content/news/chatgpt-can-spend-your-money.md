Until this month, an AI could find you a flight, compare three hotels and fill a shopping cart. Then it stopped and handed you the checkout page, because it had no way to pay.

On November 13, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, and that last step is gone. CNBC reported the details.

## How it works

You ask ChatGPT to buy something. Its shopping agent finds the item and goes to pay. It never gets your card number. The card network issues a payment token that works for that single transaction and nothing else.

You approve each purchase with a security check before any money moves. OpenAI built it with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, and says it is not taking a cut of purchases at launch.

## Who can use it

For now it is limited to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the United States who are 18 or older. No date has been given for Canada, so nobody in Alberta can use it today.

## Who is saying no

Some large sellers don't want software doing the shopping. United Airlines says it will block AI agents, and Amazon prevents agent purchases.

That fight matters more than the launch. If big retailers shut AI shoppers out, the feature stays a novelty. If they let them in, a lot of routine buying moves to software.

## What it means if you shop

Nothing changes for you this week. When it does arrive in Canada, the questions I'd ask are simple. Do I approve every purchase, or can it buy on its own? What happens when it buys the wrong thing? Who handles the refund? At launch, the answer to the first one is that you approve every purchase.

## What it means if you sell

This is the part I'd think about if I owned a store, a clinic with online booking, or any business that takes orders on a website.

A person shopping looks at photos and reads your story. Software shopping for a person reads your prices, your stock, your delivery times and your return policy. If those aren't clearly written on your site, an AI shopper can't tell its owner why to pick you.

You don't need to rebuild anything. Check that your prices, hours, service area and policies are on your website in plain text, and not only inside images or PDFs. That helps the customers you already have today, and it is what AI shoppers will read when they show up.
