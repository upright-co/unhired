# What's working (personal posts)

Rewritten every morning by the `personal-posts-review` routine from the numbers on Facebook, LinkedIn
and X (`scripts/posts-report.mjs` in the portal repo). The daily writer reads this before it picks
stories, hooks and images. Score = 5 × comments + 3 × shares + reactions, because comments are the goal.

Last updated: 2026-11-26 (13 measured posts, last 7 days)

## Do more of
- Use the story's own picture: the thing being fought over, or the people fighting it (5 source-photo
  posts avg 582, middle one 69; the 8 robot, stat, list and generated posts avg 28, best 94).
- Lead the day with an Alberta story about something being built near where people live (6 alberta
  posts avg 487 vs 29 for the 7 others; the two data centre fights scored 2,403 and 397, the other

  10 avg 19 and none passed 28).
