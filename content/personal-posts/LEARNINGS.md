# What's working (personal posts)

Rewritten every morning by the `personal-posts-review` routine from the numbers on Facebook, LinkedIn
and X (`scripts/posts-report.mjs` in the portal repo). The daily writer reads this before it picks
stories, hooks and images. Score = 5 × comments + 3 × shares + reactions, because comments are the goal.

Last updated: 2026-11-27 (20 measured posts, last 7 days)

## Do more of
- Use the story's own picture: the disputed site, a rendering of it, or the real place (5 source-photo
  posts avg 674; 6 robot posts avg 33, 6 generated avg 21; 4 of the bottom 5 were robot or generated).
- Put an Alberta story about something being built near people's homes in slots 1 to 3 (slots 1-3
  avg 582 vs 24 for slots 4-6 and 15 for 7-10; 9 alberta posts avg 381 vs 33 ai_employee, 24 ai_news).
- Open with "Would you want [a big, priced thing] near [your home, town, family]?" and a second
  sentence saying real people already pushed back, with a place name and a number (the top 2 both
  did this: 183 and 91 comments; nothing else passed 16).
- Write the caption and closing question for a Facebook neighbour, not a LinkedIn professional (20
  posts: every outside comment was on Facebook; LinkedIn shows exactly 1 comment on all 19 posts,
  his own link, 7 to 201 impressions; X 3 to 33 views).
