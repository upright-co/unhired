# What's working (personal posts)

Rewritten every morning by the `personal-posts-review` routine from the numbers on Facebook, LinkedIn
and X (`scripts/posts-report.mjs` in the portal repo). The daily writer reads this before it picks
stories, hooks and images. Score = 5 × comments + 3 × shares + reactions, because comments are the goal.

Last updated: 2026-11-28 (20 measured posts, last 7 days)

## Do more of
- Use the story's own picture: the disputed site, a rendering of it, or the real landscape (5
  source-photo posts avg 959; 6 robot avg 36, 6 generated avg 25; all 3 top posts were source photos
  of places with no faces: a campus rendering, an aerial of the Penhold site, the reservoir hills).
- Lead with an Alberta story about something being built near where people live (9 alberta posts avg
  541 vs 36 ai_employee and 26 ai_news; the 3 data-centre fights scored 2429, 1585 and 687).
- Open with "Would you want [a big, priced thing] near [your home, town, cabin, family]?" and a second
  sentence saying real people already pushed back, with a place name and a number (top 3 all did:
  184, 188 and 90 comments; nothing else passed 16).
- Put those stories in slots 1 to 3 when you have them (slots 1-3 avg 715 vs 132 for 4-6 and 17 for
  7-10, 20 posts), but don't hold one back for slot 1: Travers ran later and still reached 63K.
- Write the caption and closing question for a Facebook neighbour, not a LinkedIn professional (20
  posts: every outside comment was on Facebook; LinkedIn shows only Brandon's own link comment on all
  19 posts, 9 to 208 impressions; X 7 to 37 views, 1 like total).

## Do less of
- Robot-at-a-desk and generated stock scenes as the image (12 posts avg 30; the best were the robot
  with a man standing over it, 94, and robots in a waiting room, 8 comments, both customer questions).
- AI Employee posts about an owner's chore: who manages it, telling staff, free chatbots, physio
  rebookings (4 posts scored 10 to 20; the ones that worked asked what a customer is owed: who pays
  when AI is wrong, 94; should a business say it's an AI, 8 comments).
- Outside-Alberta AI news with no local angle (5 ai_news posts avg 26; best, the New York toy ban, 44).
