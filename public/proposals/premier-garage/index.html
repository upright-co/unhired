How the drawings stay accurate

The drawings are calculated, and the renders follow the drawings

AI image models are good at finishes and light, but they guess at measurements. So we don't ask AI to draw the plans.

  1. A drawing engine you control

    Plans and elevations are generated by code from the real numbers: width, depth, wall height, pitch, door and window sizes and positions. We'll build it on Maker.js, an open-source drawing library that outputs clean, to-scale line work and exports PDF, SVG and DXF.

  2. Your rules built in

    Your standard sizes, minimum and maximum spans, door clearances and roof options are set as limits, so a visitor can't design something you wouldn't build.

  3. Matched to your blacklines

    Line weights, title block, dimension style and notes are matched to the past drawings you send us, so the output looks like yours.

  4. Renders made from the drawing

    The exterior render uses the drawn elevation as its guide, plus reference photos of your real builds for style. The picture and the plan show the same garage.

A note on what these drawings are

The plans are accurate concept and sales drawings for quoting and for your crew's reference. They are not engineered or stamped permit drawings. Anything going to the city still goes through your normal drafting and engineering.
