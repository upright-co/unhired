Questions

What partners ask first.

Won't this cut into my own placements?

Only for orders AI can fill, and those are the ones already leaving the market. If any part of a role needs a person, the client goes back to you for that hire, and we tell them so on the first call.

What if the AI Employee can't do the role?

We check that before anything is signed. On the discovery call we split the role into its tasks and show the client which ones AI handles. If too much of the job needs a person, we say so and the order is yours.

How does the client pay?

Directly to Unhired, monthly, like a payroll line. You never invoice the client or handle the money.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Knowing which clients are struggling to fill an admin, reception, data entry or bookkeeping role is enough. We do the explaining.

Can I join if I run a virtual assistant or answering service firm?

Yes. Many of your clients need round-the-clock coverage or high-volume work your team can't price well. Refer that part to us and keep the work your people do best.
