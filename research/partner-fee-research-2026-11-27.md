## Recommendation (Option A)
20% of the client's monthly fee for the first 12 months, then 10% for as long as the client stays.
Paid after the client pays us; none if the client cancels within 90 days; lead registered before intro.
$50k role (AI Employee ≈ $2,083/mo): partner year 1 $5,000, then $2,500/yr; $10,000 by month 36 (= a 20% perm fee).
vs temp placement net ≈ $2,500/yr (3.3% net on $75,750 bill). We keep 80% in year 1, 90% after.
Caveat: our delivery cost per AI Employee isn't known; the 70% floor is gross revenue.
