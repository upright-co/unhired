# Government portals and other lead channels (research 2026-11-27)

## Government (checked 2026-11-27)
- Federal AI Source List (ITQ WS4286933967 on CanadaBuys): OPEN until 2027-09-30. Band 1 up to $1M; reportedly needs 1 delivered AI project (unconfirmed). Contact ia-ai@tpsgc-pwgsc.gc.ca. https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/ws4286933967-doc4822970058
- CanadaBuys: free SAP Ariba account. Services under $40k incl. tax can be bought directly, so pitch departments. https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/acquisitions/support-for-businesses/understand-procurement-process/how-government-buys.html
- ProServices supply arrangement: enrol in CPSS (CRA BN + Ariba ANID), quarterly refreshes, vehicle to 2028-07-04.
- Alberta Purchasing Connection (purchasing.alberta.ca): free; services thresholds $75k ministries / $100k Crowns (2024-25 table).
- City of Edmonton: SAP Business Network Discovery; low-value PO route under $75k; "Selling to the City" sessions. https://www.edmonton.ca/business_economy/selling_to_the_city/sap-ariba
- City of Calgary: Ariba, free.
- SAM.gov (US): free, needs NCAGE + UEI; Canadian firms can't bid small-business set-asides. Low fit.
- Client funding to sell with: PrairiesCan RAII (open to Dec 31 2028, SMEs <500 staff prioritized for AI adoption) https://www.canada.ca/en/prairies-economic-development/services/funding/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative.html ; BDC LIFT ($500M, loans $25k–$5M, launched Apr 2026) https://www.bdc.ca/en/about/mediaroom/news-releases/bdc-launches-lift-getting-canadian-smes-off-the-ai-sidelines ; NRC IRAP AI Assist (funds Unhired's own engine).
- Closed: CanExport SMEs (Aug 31 2026), CDAP, Alberta Jobs Now.
