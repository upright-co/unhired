01 · Who we are

An Edmonton team building AI Employees since 2021

Unhired was founded by Brandon Upright, born and raised in Edmonton. Since 2021 we've been building, training and maintaining AI Employees and custom AI systems for small and mid-sized businesses all over North America.

02 · What an AI Employee is
In one paragraph

An AI Employee is an intelligent AI built to take on a specific role and set of tasks inside your business, the way a human employee does. It works inside the tools you already use. We build it, train it on how your business operates and maintain it for you, so nobody on your team has to manage it.

Answering calls and emails
Booking appointments
Following up with customers
Keeping records and CRMs current
Bringing in and sorting new leads
Automating systems and workflows
03 · Built to empower your team

Your people keep the work only people can do

The AI Employee takes
  • The repetitive admin that fills up the day
  • Data entry and keeping systems in sync
  • Follow-ups and reminders that slip when it's busy
  • After-hours requests that would otherwise wait
Your team keeps
  • Relationships with your customers
  • Judgment calls and decisions
  • The skilled work they were hired to do
  • Final say on anything the AI prepares

Empower, not replace

Our goal is to show you first-hand how AI can empower your team, not replace them. An AI Employee takes pressure off staff who are at capacity, so a small team can handle more without burning out, and your growth doesn't have to wait on the next hire.
