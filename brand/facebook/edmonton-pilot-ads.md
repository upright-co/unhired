# Edmonton pilot: Facebook ads + video outline

Landing page: https://unhired.io/pilot?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=edmonton-pilot

Targeting: about 40 km around Edmonton, ages 30 to 65, interests in small business owners and entrepreneurship. Use the same three ads in one ad set and let Meta pick.

## Ad 1: the offer, straight

**Primary text**
Edmonton business owners: I'm choosing 5 businesses to transform with AI, completely free. I'll come to your business, sit down with your team and map every task AI can take off their plate.

Then I'll build an AI Employee for the biggest one and run it on your real work for 30 days. Free. No card, no contract.

I take 5 businesses a month and pick them from the applications.

**Headline:** Your first AI Employee, free for 30 days
**Button:** Apply now

## Ad 2: the job posting

**Primary text**
Posted a job in the last 90 days?

Before you hire, let me show you how much of that role an AI Employee can do. I'll visit your business, interview the people doing the work now, and put an AI Employee on the biggest task for a month at no cost.

If you keep it after 30 days, it costs no more than half what the role pays a person. If not, I switch it off.

5 Edmonton spots a month.

**Headline:** Before you hire, try an AI Employee free
**Button:** Apply now

## Ad 3: the missed calls

**Primary text**
Who answers your phone at 12:15 and after 5?

For a lot of Edmonton businesses, nobody does. I'll come in, watch how your day runs and build an AI Employee that handles the calls, emails or bookings your team can't get to. It works on your real customers for 30 days, free.

Apply in 2 minutes. I pick 5 businesses a month.

**Headline:** I'll set up your first AI Employee in person
**Button:** Apply now

## Video outline (about 2 minutes, filmed by Brandon)

1. **Hook (0 to 10 s).** "If you run a business in Edmonton and you've posted a job in the last few months, give me two minutes."
2. **Who you are (10 to 25 s).** Your name, that you run Unhired here in Edmonton, and that you do every visit yourself. Use only real details.
3. **The offer (25 to 60 s).** Walk through it: the visit and team interviews, the AI Opportunity Map, the build, 30 days on real work, the day-30 review.
4. **Why it's free (60 to 80 s).** You want them to see it working on their own business before they pay. If it earns its place, they'll keep it. If it doesn't, you switch it off.
5. **The catch (80 to 100 s).** You need a few hours of their time, the owner there for the visit and a team that will talk to you. Say who it's not for.
6. **Ask (100 to 120 s).** "I take 5 businesses a month. Hit Apply under this video. It takes two minutes, and I read every one."

When it's filmed, upload it to YouTube as unlisted (or send the .mp4). Then set `pilotConfig.videoUrl` in `config.ts` and the video takes the hero spot.
