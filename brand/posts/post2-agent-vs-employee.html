AI Employee 101

AI agent vs.
AI Employee

Same technology underneath. One is a tool you use. The other is a job it holds.

AI agentAI Employee
ScopeA task you hand itA whole role it owns
Starts whenSomeone prompts itThe phone rings, a form comes in, a bill goes overdue
MemoryOften starts freshKnows your business and keeps context
AuthorityWhatever access it getsClear rules: act, act and report, or ask first
OwnerWhoever set it up, if anyoneSomeone accountable for training and fixing it
